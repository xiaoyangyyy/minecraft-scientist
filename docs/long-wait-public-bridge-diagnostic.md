safe-seed, compound-motor, long-WAIT and support-loss capture schemas. Required

capture's source binding and its saved source snapshot. Public metadata, event
journal, images and diagnostic source bytes are hashed before/after consumption.

attestation only; private camera/player numerical values are not inputs.

All original completed selected-anchor pairs are replayed in epoch/trial order.
Direct tracks, first-image seed pixels/denominator, cells, displacement summaries
and selection/support decisions must reproduce the original sample exactly.
Continuous comparison retains the same immutable first-image seeds and consumes
every actual image, without reseeding, resurrection, skipped failures or altered
gates. Intervals exceeding the original 128-image tracking bound remain explicitly
unavailable, never shortened. Exact integer control ordinals must form the
original contiguous journal with anchor at most 48 and final action at most 288.
Trailing compliant actions are preserved separately, not invented as a completed
trial. No original record or native artifact is replaced.

## Consequence for the next intervention

Wholesale substitution of this unchanged continuous tracker is not supported.
More stable waiting alone cannot restore identities already lost during motion.
Public frame-57/58 inspection shows a large perspective/vertical image change
against nearby repeated dirt/leaf texture, without control-compliance failure.
That does not isolate repetition, occlusion, jump dynamics or tracker convergence
as a uniquely proven cause.

The next declared intervention should test controllable **sustained** view change
instead of another one-tick motor/longer-WAIT tweak: maintain one anonymous
compound handle for a fixed bounded multi-tick stride, preserve every one-tick
receipt, and retain the original settling/seed/geometry/future gates and global
288-action cap. Both acquisition and future motor duration need explicit schema
and auditor registration; parent micro-control output cannot represent it.
Fresh separately audited development captures, not gains on these known images,
must decide whether this supplies useful stable baselines. If it still fails,
first-image texture ambiguity and observability-aware view selection remain
hypotheses to test, not established fixes.

All original completion gates in `docs/quality-roadmap.md` remain required,
including actual arrival, disjoint held-out worlds/conditions, integrated natural
science/map operation, long recovery, comparisons and consolidated release.
