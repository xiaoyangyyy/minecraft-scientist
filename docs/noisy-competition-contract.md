# Noisy competing theories: finite-family experimental contract

Date: 2026-10-10. New development stage, not formal statistical preregistration
or original-method proof. Prior stochastic execution supplied one correct model;
here the Agent gets 18 competing factor combinations and not the selected truth.
No old 211/223 method/runtime dependency is changed while legacy audit runs.

## Models, evidence and inference

Use the shared TheoryIR for every factor and joint executable program. Mechanism
templates: ordinary activation, extra wait and activation not required. Controller
templates: healthy, memoryless half-drop of primary activation, or correlated
two-mode delivery. Sensor templates: noiseless or symmetric 1/8 flips. Candidate
IDs are opaque, definitions and priors identical across private conditions.
The extra 1/2 sensor condition is deliberately outside this family. These are
declared finite templates, NOT discovery of arbitrary unknown control structures.

All combinations initially have equal prior mass. For public action/output,
joint-state output mass yields each model likelihood, Bayesian model weights
and within-model conditional next-state belief. Eliminate only zero likelihood,
not a noisy disagreement with a single deterministic prediction. All-zero model
mass is mismatch and does not silently reset. Physical reset resets latent state
only, retaining all model evidence; it is not an implicit new stationary epoch.
Probability bounds fail transactionally, with the acquired actual record retained.

Factor identifiers cannot redefine their IR. Model copies/cache results are
detached from caller mutation. Project factor mass over ALL candidates, retaining
equal-behavior factor ambiguity. A high posterior is conditional on the supplied
language/prior; it does not establish physical identifiability or calibrated
confidence, particularly for a family with positive support for every observation.
No theory is promoted; accepted_theory/accepted_theories remain null.

## Experiment universe and policies

At each decision enumerate every legal word of length 1..H, H<=4, in the declared
action order, for continuation and reset modes, within remaining actual budget.
Reset costs 1; each atomic command has a positive public integer cost. Prediction
conditions on current per-model belief for continuation, initial belief for reset.
Action spelling is not a ranking feature. Commit the complete chosen word before
its actions; freeze per-action forecasts before dispatch. No free reset/validation.

Joint information gain is sum(i,y) w_i P_i(y) log2(P_i(y)/P_mix(y)), divided by
query cost. It is an established strong finite-family design baseline, not a new
method. Logarithms use Decimal precision 50; only tiny negative roundoff within
1e-40 is clamped, never likelihoods/posteriors. Exact distinguishability compares
output distributions, not rounded entropy or program strings.

Mechanism criterion uses zero-one Bayes risk 1-max_m P(m), less its expected
posterior risk, divided by cost. MI is a secondary criterion when risk ties;
joint MI similarly uses mechanism risk as secondary. Both are one-query design
criteria, not optimal full-budget policies. Repeated equal output distributions
reuse scores at the same posterior; the full word universe is not filtered.

Uniform-word samples uniformly among ALL feasible (word,reset) pairs, including
uninformative words. It is not uniform independent primitive actions. All three
share priors, horizon, cost budget and capability inputs. Default budget 96 is
this explicitly new prototype protocol, not a reduced legacy budget or final
framework acceptance setting. Legacy 1792-source experiment budgets remain intact.

## Complete matrix and auditing

Two seeds/naming mappings, five stationary conditions (no change, control only,
mechanism only, both, unmodeled sensor), all three policies: 30 actual Agent runs.
These conditions diagnose current factors relative to a PUBLIC template family;
no paid old-model acquisition or unannounced online mutation is claimed here.
Naming/seeds are not independent structural holdouts or statistical samples.

After policy execution, freeze public ledger before obtaining evaluator truth.
Save partial actual evidence on execution failures; never finish a failed batch.
All complete public replays of the declared policies/costs/predictions/posteriors
occur before any selected private audit/scoring. Private raw replay uses the
independent component executor contract and the replayed adaptive dispatches.
Verify complete selection, source pins, artifact coverage, configuration equality,
constructed private spec, actual clocks/controls, all rows and denominators.

Report all outcomes, costs, query/kernel counts and factor masses. A fixed .9
posterior support indicator is exploratory prior-conditional support, not a
confidence interval or theory certification. Incorrect support, ties, abstention,
unmodeled truth and remaining ambiguity stay in the complete report; do not
present null accepted theories as successful zero-error revision. Compute timing
covers actual policy only; total audit/orchestration remains distinct/unmeasured.

Limits: <=32 candidates, <=256 inference observations, rational representation
<=12000 bits, <=512 complete words, <=200000 kernel calls and <=16384 reset-cache
entries. Each prediction also respects prior stochastic runtime bounds. Exhaustion
is explicit, not horizon/candidate truncation. All-positive family likelihood does
not imply family adequacy; calibration, valid noisy stopping/shadow validation and
unknown-structure generation remain separate required work.

Main-interpreter integration, formal structural/statistical design, faithful
adaptive/atomic-random/LLM matrices, online version revision, H4/negative transfer,
controlled Minecraft, natural RGB/navigation/long recovery and release remain open.
