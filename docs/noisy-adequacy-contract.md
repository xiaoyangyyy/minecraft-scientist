## Question and mathematical assumptions

High posterior support only compares supplied candidates and can be wrong when
the entire family is wrong. Test the fixed stationary family as a composite null.
For predictable commands/resets and observed output prefixes, let L_i(t) be the
cumulative conditional likelihood of fixed public model i, including correctly
modeled hidden state and reset initial distributions. Let U(t)=K^(-t) be the
predictable uniform public-output alternative. Use E(t)=U(t)/max_i L_i(t).

For each true model j, E(t) is dominated by U(t)/L_j(t), a likelihood-ratio test
supermartingale under the declared law. A crossing of 1/alpha controls a single
trajectory's any-time family false rejection at alpha. The pointwise minimum
need not itself be a supermartingale. Source: [Universal Inference, section 8,
Theorem 9 and conditional-likelihood extension](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.11436).
This is an existing construction, not new statistical theory.

Assumptions matter: family fixed before observations; correct full conditional
output/control/reset law for at least one null; predictable action/reset choices
and alternative; no unmodeled public side channel governing the policy. Current
Agent uses signals and fixed public commands/costs. Reset initial observation is
deterministic and ancillary under this fixture contract. A deterministic PRNG
seed, successful trace replay or few development replications do not prove the
stochastic-law assumption or an empirical calibrated error rate.

No prior enters the null likelihood maximum; a prior mixture may be unsafe for
individual null members. Also max_i product_t P_i is not product_t max_i P_i:
switching the null model freely at every step changes the tested family. Resets
clear latent state, not L_i, U, alpha, counters or historical rejection evidence.

Default alpha=1/20 is fixed for this new development run, after prior development
results were known. It is per trajectory, NOT a simultaneous .05 claim over all
30 runs, extra epochs, revised families or selected alpha values. Prospective
formal evaluation still needs its own frozen families, error allocation and
independent structural sampling. Uniform alternative has no universal power or
guaranteed model-mismatch detection. Nonrejection is not a passing adequacy test.

## Implementation and actual evidence

Before actual dispatch, freeze all fixed-model conditional output distributions
and the alternative probability. Afterwards propagate exact cumulative likelihood
and conditional hidden state, retaining all observations. Zero-probability null
prefixes remain zero after resets; all-zero null is explicit JSON-tagged infinity,
never a floating NaN/Infinity or implicit model recovery. Rejection is sticky,
with first action index and all actual reset/action costs recorded.

The shadow wrapper does not feed this diagnostic into the planner, shorten its
budget or change its prior. It executes identical paid actions to the baseline,
including after rejection, so this stage can measure detection without claiming
it has repaired the theory. No registry changes, new structure or theory promotion.
Partial Agent/monitor/raw receipts survive failures; failed batches have no final
manifest. Storage failures preserve existing bytes, not impossible extra writes.

Limits: one to32 public models, <=256 observations, <=12000 exact coefficient
bits, <=2 million actual monitor prediction branches. No candidate pruning except
mathematically zero prefix likelihood; all candidates remain reported. Exhaustion
fails without approximate arithmetic or silently restarting the statistical test.
Branch counts are monitor prediction traversals, not all inference/Python cost.
Timing covers actual Agent plus online monitor, not isolated marginal overhead;
public replay, raw audit, storage and orchestration are excluded and unmeasured.

Independent monitor checker uses unnormalized raw IR state vectors carrying the
entire likelihood; reset multiplies initial distribution by previous likelihood.
It reconstructs every public forecast, reset, e-value, running maximum, rejection
and actual cost. No monitor update/class or compiled prediction/filter calls.
Shared schema validation, scope text and original core public-policy verifier are
explicitly reused. The running maximum is a crossing summary, NOT itself an
e-process/e-value. The checker verifies monitor mechanics, not physical causality
or a measured frequentist guarantee in real Minecraft.
