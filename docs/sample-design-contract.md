# Sample precision and power: pre-data arithmetic contract

Date: 2026-10-10. This contract is a design aid, **not formal preregistration**.
Previously disclosed development outcomes cannot become unseen evaluation data.
No sealed input is read, and no new scientific world is generated by this tool.

## Sampling unit and scope

Define a binary endpoint on each IID sampling unit from a specified target world
distribution. For example, a group failure may mean any wrong promotion within a
fixed group assessment, but group membership/size/conditions and how unresolved,
rejected, no-anomaly and ineligible cases count must be fixed beforehand. A low
wrong-promotion rate obtained by never promoting cannot establish discovery:
coverage and correct recovery require separate prespecified endpoints.

Leakage groups are dependence-control partitions, not proof of random independent
draws. Repeated IDs, seeds or analyses do not increase n. Equal-weight group means
of continuous/proportion metrics are not automatically Bernoulli observations.
The tool therefore does not compute inferential bounds from current group reports.

## Exact fixed-sample construction

Let p be group failure probability, n the fixed number of IID groups, k the fixed
maximum allowed failed groups, r the unacceptable risk ceiling, alpha the family
error allowance, and m the prespecified number of endpoint statements. Passing
means X <= k. The binomial lower tail is

    F(n,k,p) = sum(j=0..k) choose(n,j) p^j (1-p)^(n-j).

Choose n,k such that F(n,k,r) <= alpha/m. For any p >= r the pass probability is
no greater, so this gives a one-sided fixed-sample risk test. Bonferroni controls
the probability of any false statement in the fixed family by alpha; independence
between endpoints is not required, but each endpoint's sample model must hold.
See [NIST exact binomial limits](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbici.htm)
and [NIST Bonferroni coverage](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc463.htm).
The formulas here are the binomial/union-bound specialization, not a new method.

For zero failures the upper endpoint is 1-(alpha/m)^(1/n). At alpha=.05 and r=.05,
the smallest zero-failure n is 59 for m=1 and 99 for m=8. These are arithmetic
examples under the assumptions, not evidence that existing data satisfy them.

Zero-failure designs may have poor pass probability at a good but nonzero rate.
The powered search additionally requires F(n,k,p_good) >= power, with p_good < r.
It searches k in increasing order. For each k the least admissible n is found
by exact monotone tail inversion. At fixed k power decreases with n, and minimal
n(k) increases with k; thus the first feasible k gives a minimum n among these
fixed-sample lower-tail designs in the declared resource bounds. No claim is made
about optimality among sequential, randomized, comparative or other test designs.
Power is **marginal per endpoint**, not the probability all m endpoints pass.

All decisions use exact rational integer tails; display brackets are outward
rounded. Inputs reject booleans/floats and invalid domains. Limits are n<=5000,
k<=64, m<=128, input denominator<=1000000 and <=2000 tail evaluations per design.
Out-of-bound inputs raise; exhausted finite search reports no design within the
declared limits, not impossibility. No clipping/truncating/optional stopping.

## What remains before formal evaluation

The 1/5/10% risk grid, m=1/4/8 and 80% marginal power at r/4 are sensitivity values,
not chosen acceptance thresholds. Formal planning still needs a defensible world
distribution and independence construction, fixed structural split/assessment,
primary outcomes and scientifically meaningful effects/risks, safety/coverage
tradeoffs, full multiplicity family, comparative paired power/precision, missing
and control-failure rules, frozen methods/strong baselines/budgets and analysis.
Current 18 sealed groups cannot simply be declared sufficient or IID by this
calculation. Do not access them to pick an attractive endpoint or threshold.

This tool advances the statistical-design gate only. It does not close unknown
control/noise, strong baselines, H4/negative transfer, controlled Minecraft,
natural RGB, navigation, long recovery or integrated release gates.
